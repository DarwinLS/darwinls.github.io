Bio

Background

Hi there! I'm a software engineer and product designer, fresh out of UC San Diego with a B.S. in Mathematics and Computer Science. I designed and built Veraflux, where a curiosity about clinical research met the engineering: a product that turns PubMed studies into supplement reports anyone can actually read, with sources cited. I made the product and design decisions and built every layer, from the React and TypeScript front end to the retrieval-augmented Python pipeline and the infrastructure behind it.

It started as my own problem. Before trying a supplement I'd try to find and read the studies myself, but the ones that mattered for me were buried in an ocean of PubMed publications, and I couldn't reliably tell when an article's writer was biased. I wanted a tool that condensed what I needed and showed its sources, so I built one.

I've also worked as a test engineer for SiteHive, an Australian IoT company, running firmware updates and connectivity diagnostics on their Hexanode devices and validating each unit before shipment to customers.

I like work that crosses engineering, design, and product, building end to end. Getting into unfamiliar domains and sweating the details until something feels polished is when I do my best work. I'm looking for full-stack, infrastructure, and applied-AI engineering roles, and product design roles, but I'm open to anything I can be excited about.
