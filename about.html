About

Full-Stack & Applied-AI Engineer. Founder of Veraflux.

I am a full-stack and applied-AI engineer. I build complete products, and I am most useful where the hard part is invisible: the retrieval, the correctness, and the infrastructure that has to stay up.

Most of my work right now is Veraflux, a production research platform I designed, built, shipped, and operate on my own. It started with a habit. I always read the studies myself before trying a supplement, and wished for an unbiased tool that just condensed them. A genuine curiosity about clinical research met the engineering, and the build followed.

What I am proudest of is not any single feature. It is that I owned every layer, from product strategy and design through the React front end and the retrieval-augmented Python pipeline to the infrastructure behind it, and that the promises the product makes are enforced in code rather than in copy.
