About

Full-Stack & Applied-AI Engineer. Founder of Veraflux.

I'm a full-stack and applied-AI engineer. I'm most in my element when there's room for creativity across engineering, design, and product, and the range is the point: I'd rather own the whole arc of a build than any single slice of it.

Most of my work right now is Veraflux, a production research platform I designed, built, shipped, and operate on my own. It started with a habit. I always read the studies myself before trying a supplement, and wished for an unbiased tool that just condensed them, so I built one. That curiosity about clinical research is one interest of many, but it's the one that turned into a product.

What I'm proudest of is not any single feature. It's that I owned every layer, from product strategy and design through the React front end and the retrieval-augmented Python pipeline to the infrastructure behind it, and that the promises the product makes are enforced in code rather than in copy.

Getting into unfamiliar domains and sweating the details until something feels genuinely polished is when I do my best work.
