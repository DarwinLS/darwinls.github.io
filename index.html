Engineering

How Veraflux is built
Applied AI

A pipeline that cites only what it was given

13 model stages across Gemini and Claude, and a server check that drops any citation outside the studies the model was given.
Reliability

Deadlines in front of a slow backend

Time budgets that start when a request arrives, and load shedding that happens before any quota or money is spent.
Infrastructure

A schema that could not be rebuilt

Replayed 31 migrations into an empty database, then matched production's columns and indexes line for line.

Design

Color, accessibility, typography

Evidence you can trust, typeset honestly

A rating badge that made thin evidence look like a safety warning, a brand color that failed as text, and type choices made for a report full of statistics.
Interaction design

A scan flow built around the wait

Photograph a supplement label and wait minutes for research on every ingredient, with the list staying on screen and a clock instead of a fake progress bar.
Content design

Error and pricing copy that tells the truth

Error messages that said what happened, a free plan that undersold the product, and a price line that told subscribers their bill would rise.
